Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the website you are reading and Lotse, the private publishing tool used to run the Unit-141 channel. Lotse is operated by Shady Abdou, only for the Unit-141 accounts. Contact: shadyabdou.du@gmail.com.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What Lotse accesses

Lotse only connects to accounts that belong to the channel owner, after the owner signs in and grants access:

  • YouTube (Google): uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own channel, sets their thumbnails, reads comments on those videos, posts the replies the owner approves, and reads their view, like and comment counts.
  • Instagram (Meta): publishes the owner's Reels, reads and answers comments on those posts, and reads the posts' insights.
  • TikTok: sends the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account (as drafts or posts) and reads those videos' view counts.

What is stored and where

Everything stays on the owner's own server: the access tokens for the owner's accounts, the scheduled posts and their media, comments on the owner's own posts (with the commenter's public username and text) for up to 90 days, and view counts. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising.

When the owner asks an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to help write captions or replies, the text of the posts and comments involved is sent to that assistant for that purpose only.

Google user data

Lotse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Lotse uses the YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your rights

If you commented on a Unit-141 post and want your comment data deleted from Lotse, write to shadyabdou.du@gmail.com and it will be removed. Lotse never sends direct messages, never follows or likes on its own and never collects data about people beyond public comments on the channel's own posts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
